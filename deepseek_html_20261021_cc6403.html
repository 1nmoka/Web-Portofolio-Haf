03 / METHODOLOGY

The 3-Step Iteration Framework

A systematic operational trajectory ensuring analytical rigor remains directly tied to real-world civic impact and clear stakeholder decisions.

01. Phase One

OBSERVE

Ground truth collection, sensory field observation, high-resolution drone reconnaissance, and participatory urban mapping sessions with community representatives.

OUTPUT: Raw GeoTIFFs, Interview Matrices, Unprocessed Point Clouds
02. Phase Two

ANALYZE

Spatial multi-criteria analysis (MCA), network flow density simulations, topological error correction, and cross-variable correlation models.

OUTPUT: Isochrone Models, Cost-Distance Rasters, Sensitivity Indices
03. Phase Three

SYNTHESIZE

High-fidelity masterplan synthesis, actionable spatial policy documentation, interactive dashboard publication, and executive stakeholder translation.

OUTPUT: Publication Atlases, WebGIS Portals, Statutory Policy Briefs
